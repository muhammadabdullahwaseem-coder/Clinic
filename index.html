About the Doctor

Meet Dr. Ahmed Raza

Dr. Ahmed Raza is a highly qualified General Physician and Family Medicine Specialist with over a decade of clinical experience. He earned his MBBS from King Edward Medical University and completed his FCPS in Family Medicine, bringing world-class expertise to every consultation. His patient-first approach has earned him the trust of thousands of families across Lahore.

  • MBBS – King Edward Medical University, Lahore
  • FCPS (Family Medicine) – College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan
  • Former Senior Registrar at Services Hospital Lahore
  • Holistic, evidence-based care with a focus on preventive medicine

What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive medical care tailored to every stage of life — for you and your entire family.

General Checkup

Thorough head-to-toe examination to assess your overall health and catch issues early.

Child Healthcare

Pediatric consultations, growth monitoring, and illness management for children of all ages.

Vaccinations

Recommended immunizations for children and adults following the latest health guidelines.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and other long-term conditions.

Lab Tests

In-house diagnostic lab for blood work, urine analysis, and other essential tests.

Home Visits

Doctor visits at your doorstep for patients who are unable to travel to the clinic.
